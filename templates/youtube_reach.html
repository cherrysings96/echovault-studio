How the EchoVault YouTube Reach Algorithm Works

Cover songs on YouTube succeed through a balance of search demand and audience retention. EchoVault calculates the reach probability across four weighted vectors:

1. Trend Velocity (35%)

Measures breakout velocity versus evergreen search intent across regional audio trends.

2. Arrangement Novelty (25%)

Stripped acoustic, cinematic, or unique piano re-arrangements retain watch-time longer than 1:1 replica backing tracks.

3. Saturation Index (20%)

Applies an algorithmic penalty if high competition crowds out organic recommendations.

4. Live Mix-Readiness (20%)

Directly queries your studio SQLite takes table—higher pitch and vibe ratings increase viral retention.
